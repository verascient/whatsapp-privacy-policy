Verascient Assistant privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who operates the assistant

Verascient Limited, registered in England and Wales under company number 16818464, operates Verascient Assistant. Our registered office is 20 Wenlock Road, London, N1 7GU, United Kingdom. Contact contact@verascient.com for privacy questions and deletion requests.

The assistant lets people ask questions about their Verascient knowledge graph through WhatsApp. It is currently being configured and is not yet open for live use.

Information we process

We process the text you send, our replies, message and conversation identifiers, timestamps, delivery status, and technical usage and error information. WhatsApp may supply your phone number, a business-scoped identifier, and optional profile details. To answer a question, the assistant processes relevant information from the knowledge graph that your linked Verascient account is permitted to access, together with a limited amount of conversation history.

The assistant is configured for text messages. If you send attachments or voice messages, WhatsApp and Kapso may still receive and store them even though this assistant does not use them to answer questions.

How we use information

We use this information to verify your account linkage, retrieve permitted knowledge, generate and deliver answers, maintain conversation context, prevent duplicate processing, and investigate delivery or service failures. Knowledge access requires linking your WhatsApp identity to your Verascient account through an ownership-verification message. Your account's existing permissions govern access.

Providing a WhatsApp identity and message is necessary to use this channel. You may instead use Verascient directly. AI-generated answers can be incorrect and should be checked against their sources.

Services involved

  • Meta and WhatsApp deliver messages and process WhatsApp account and messaging information under their applicable terms and privacy policies.
  • Kapso connects the WhatsApp business account, stores conversation history, and delivers messages and webhooks. We have disabled optional model and product improvement for this assistant's Kapso project.
  • Verascient Platform verifies account linkage, enforces knowledge permissions, and stores message-processing and reply-recovery records.
  • Google Cloud supplies the AI model in the current setup. Message text, selected conversation history, and relevant permitted knowledge are sent to the model to generate an answer. Provider processing and retention are governed by its service terms and configured features.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this policy. GitHub may process visitors' technical information under its own privacy statement. This page adds no analytics scripts or tracking cookies.

Provider infrastructure may process information outside your country. Contact us for information about the hosting and provider arrangements applicable to your workspace. Relevant provider notices include WhatsApp's privacy policy, Kapso's privacy policy, Google Cloud's privacy notice, and GitHub's privacy statement.

Storage and retention

The bot has no separate application database. Platform's message-processing and reply-recovery records are configured for deletion after seven days by scheduled cleanup. Account linkage remains until unlinked or removed. Knowledge-graph records have their own workspace retention rules and are not deleted by conversation cleanup. Tool exchanges are held temporarily during an individual model run.

The current Kapso Free project keeps conversation history indefinitely unless it is deleted. Its diagnostic records and other providers' logs, security records, and backups follow separate retention practices. Platform cleanup does not automatically delete those copies. We do not promise zero retention across all providers.

Request deletion

Email contact@verascient.com with the subject "WhatsApp data deletion" and enough information to identify your conversation, such as your sender identifier and approximate dates. Do not send passwords or verification codes. We may verify that the request concerns you.

We will assess the request, remove the relevant records we control where applicable, and handle any necessary requests to Kapso or other providers. We will explain any legal or technical limits that prevent complete deletion. Deleting WhatsApp conversation records does not by itself delete your organisation's underlying knowledge graph.

Your choices and rights

You can stop messaging the assistant and unlink your WhatsApp identity in Verascient. Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of your personal data. Contact us to exercise those rights. You can also raise a complaint with your local data-protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

We will update this page when the assistant's data handling changes, including before opening it for live use, and show the latest update date above.
